# The photo booth

Press <kbd>⌃⌥⌘B</kbd>, or click **大头贴** (photo booth) in the notch panel.

1. **The curtain closes.** A pleated terracotta velvet curtain drops over the screen. (If Nocchi does not have camera permission yet, macOS asks first, before the curtain appears.)
2. **Choose a frame.** Behind the curtain, a brass-trimmed showcase holds a sample strip of the selected frame.
3. **Drop a coin.** Press the glowing **开始** (start) button. Only now does the camera turn on.
4. **Look at the lens.** 请看镜头！ Then 3, 2, 1, and a white flash, four times.
5. **The curtain opens** and the strip comes out of the notch.

## Choosing a frame

- **Frames**: the brass arrows (or <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>) go through eleven frames. First the six booth themes: 老照相亭 (classic booth, with a serial number), 黑白照相亭 (black booth), 情人节 (hearts, with heart-shaped windows and lace), 金星 (gold stars on ink blue), 生日快乐 (birthday bunting) and 新年快乐 (new year, gold on vermilion). Then the general templates that suit booth paper: 经典 (classic), 炭黑 (charcoal), 牛皮纸 (kraft), 胶片框 (film frame) and 70 年代条纹 (70s stripes).
- **四格条 / 四宫格**: a chrome lever switches between a four-frame strip and a 2×2 grid (or press <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd>). Every frame exists in both layouts.
- **黑白 / 彩色**: the other lever chooses black and white or color. Black and white uses *Booth Silver 400*, unless a black-and-white roll is loaded; color uses *Booth Flash 400*, unless you loaded a color film of your own. The film is named under the levers.
- <kbd>Return</kbd> starts; <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves.

The sample strip is composed by the same code as the real print, so what you see is what comes out. Your choice is remembered for next time, and you can also set it in *Settings → 相纸*.

## Leaving

You can leave at any moment: the **离开大头贴机** (leave the photo booth) button at the top left, <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or <kbd>⌘Q</kbd>. The curtain stays below the menu bar, so *Quit* in the menu bar always works. Choosing a frame never times out; once you press start, the session gives up after a minute if something goes wrong.
