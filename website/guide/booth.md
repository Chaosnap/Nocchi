# The photo booth

Press <kbd>⌃⌥⌘B</kbd>, or click **Photo Booth** in the notch panel.

1. **The curtain closes.** A pleated, deep-red theatre velvet curtain with a gold-fringed valance drops over the screen and sways gently. (If Nocchi does not have camera permission yet, macOS asks first, before the curtain appears.)
2. **Choose a frame.** Behind the curtain stands the booth: walnut and brass under a marquee of bulbs, with a sample strip of the selected frame in its window.
3. **Drop a coin.** Press the glowing red **Start** button. Only now does the camera turn on.
4. **Look at the camera!** Then 3, 2, 1, and a white flash, four times.
5. **The curtain opens** and the strip comes out of the notch.

## Choosing a frame

- **Frames**: the brass arrows (or <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>) go through eleven frames. First the six booth themes: *Classic Booth* (with a serial number), *B&W Booth*, *Valentine* (heart-shaped windows and lace), *Gold Stars* (on ink blue), *Happy Birthday* (bunting) and *Happy New Year* (gold on vermilion). Then the general templates that suit booth paper: *Classic*, *Charcoal*, *Kraft*, *Film Frame* and *Seventies Stripes*.
- **Strip / Grid**: a chrome lever switches between a four-frame strip and a 2×2 grid (or press <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd>). Every frame exists in both layouts.
- **B&W / Colour**: the other lever chooses black and white or colour. Black and white uses *Booth Silver 400*, unless a black-and-white roll is loaded; color uses *Booth Flash 400*, unless you loaded a color film of your own. The film is named under the levers.
- <kbd>Return</kbd> starts; <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves.

The sample strip is composed by the same code as the real print, so what you see is what comes out. Your choice is remembered for next time, and you can also set it in *Settings → Paper*.

## Leaving

You can leave at any moment: the **Leave the Photo Booth** button at the top left, <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or <kbd>⌘Q</kbd>. The curtain stays below the menu bar, so *Quit* in the menu bar always works. Choosing a frame never times out; once you press start, the session gives up after a minute if something goes wrong.
