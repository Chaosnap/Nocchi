# Prints, the shelf and the album

## Prints on the desktop

New prints drift into a pile at the top right of the screen, the newest on top. Drag one anywhere; near the edge of the screen it snaps on like a fridge magnet. Right-click a print for:

- **装饰…** (decorate) and the [quick notes](./decorating#quick-notes-on-the-desktop);
- **在 Finder 中显示** (show in Finder), **用「预览」打开** (open in Preview), **拷贝** (copy), **共享…** (share);
- **贴到桌面层 / 浮到最上层**: pin the print behind your windows, on the desktop layer, or float it above them again;
- **收进相册** (put it in the album) and **移到废纸篓…** (move to the Trash).

Prints stay where you left them when you quit and open Nocchi again.

## The notch shelf

Drag a print onto the notch and it tucks into the notch. Hover over the notch and choose **暂存** (shelf) to see the stack: drag a finished print out into any app, click one to put it back on the desktop, put them all back, or show them in the Finder.

## The album

<kbd>⌘0</kbd>, the Dock icon, or the menu bar opens the album. The four views are in the tray at the top, or <kbd>⌘1</kbd>–<kbd>⌘4</kbd>.

### 软木板 · Corkboard

A cork board in a walnut frame. Each month gets an embossed label (2026 · 十一月) and a typed note; prints are pinned in a loose collage with brass pins, map pins, kraft tape or wooden clothespins, and months with three or more prints get a ticket stub, a stamp or a strip of film.

![The corkboard](/screenshots/album-corkboard.jpg)

### 卡片盒 · Card box

A beechwood index-card box with 27 dividers. By default each print is filed under the first letter of its caption (A–Z; Chinese captions by their pinyin, prints without a caption under #). Hover a divider to see how many prints it holds; tabs with prints have an orange dot. Click it and the cards slide one by one out of their own slot and land in a loose pile on the right (up to nine; drag sideways to leaf through, or drag cards anywhere and stack them). Click a card to bring it to the front, double-click to open it, and **全部放回** (put them all back) files them again.

![The card box](/screenshots/album-cardbox.jpg)

**Filing by date**: the gear next to **＋** at the top right files the box by the **year, month or day** a picture was taken instead. The divider tabs change to *2026*, *Nov* or *11/24*, newest at the front; with more than 27 groups, the oldest share the last divider, *Older*. The cards' own little tabs change too.

![The card box filed by month](/screenshots/album-cardbox-month.jpg)

### 胶卷 · Film

An aluminium light table on a walnut desk. Prints are laid out four at a time as vertical 35 mm strips, with sprocket holes and orange edge markings; booth strips go on whole. Hover a frame for a 2.2× loupe, **洗一洗** (shuffle) reshuffles, and **正片 / 负片** switches between positive and negative.

![The film light table](/screenshots/album-film.jpg)

### 日历 · Calendar

A wall calendar printed in red and black, with brass page turners. Days with photos carry a small print in photo corners (a red stamp shows *+2* when there are more), and today is circled in red. Click a day to see its prints; the footer counts the month's prints and rolls and your most-used tag.

![The wall calendar](/screenshots/album-calendar.jpg)

### Search

<kbd>⌘F</kbd> searches every view: captions, tags (with or without #), places, film names in Chinese or English, dates (2026, 11月, 2026/11/23), the pinyin of Chinese captions, or a single first letter. In the English interface, English month names work too (nov, November). Every word must match. The card box draws the matching cards out of the box; the calendar turns to the month of the latest match.
