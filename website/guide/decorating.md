# The decorating desk

## Quick notes on the desktop

Right-click a print on the desktop for:

- **在白边上写字** (write on the border): write with your trackpad; the line follows the pressure. Choose **写完了** (done) when you finish.
- **写一句题字…** (write a caption): one typed line on the border. The default caption is 我可以见你吗？ (*may I see you?*); change it in *Settings → 相纸*.
- **印上日期 / 不印日期** (print the date or not) and **擦掉笔迹** (erase the handwriting).

## The decorating desk

Double-click a print, or right-click it and choose **装饰…** (decorate). The same works in every album view. You can also have the desk open by itself whenever a print finishes developing: *Settings → 相纸 → 显影完成后自动打开装饰台*.

![The decorating desk](/screenshots/decorate.jpg)

A dark walnut desk under a warm lamp. Everything you do is stored with the photo, not burned into it: you can change the template, move a sticker or erase a line at any time, and **完成** (done) composes the finished print again from the negative.

### Tools (left)

A leather tray with select, **pen**, **marker**, **oil crayon** (the grease pencil used on contact sheets), **paint pen** (opaque, for writing white or gold on dark photos) and **eraser** (removes the stroke you click). Eight enamel colors, three sizes, undo and redo. You can draw on the photo and on the border.

### Stickers

Six drawers of printed stickers, all drawn by Nocchi: vinyl **badges** (sun, rain, coffee, dog, cat, enamel heart, camera, film…), **rubber stamps** (a postmark with the date, OK!, LOVE, 已显影 *developed*, a red 珍藏 *treasured* seal…), **ephemera** (a cinema ticket, a postage stamp, a price tag, a luggage tag, film, a receipt, a matchbox label), **washi tape**, **embossed labels** you can type on, and **die-cut emoji**. Drag a sticker to move it; the handle at its corner scales and rotates it. Stickers that take text show a text field; some can change color; any can be flipped, brought to the front or deleted.

### Templates

Every template that fits this paper, previewed live with your own photo.

### Text

A caption or mood; the caption type (手写 handwriting, 打字机 typewriter, 铅字 letterpress); the date and time the photo was taken (you can change it) and whether to print the date; a place; tags, from suggestions or your own. You can choose to print the tags and the place on the border, and to add a small matching sticker for tags it knows (#阳光 *sunshine* gets a sun badge, #咖啡 *coffee* a coffee cup).

### Share

A 1080 × 1350 poster of the print on linen, walnut, kraft paper, velvet, cork, 70s stripes, a clear sky or darkroom red, centered, slightly tilted or as a pair, with an optional line of small type. Export it as JPEG or PNG, copy it, or share it.

### Keys

<kbd>⌘Z</kbd> / <kbd>⇧⌘Z</kbd> undo and redo, <kbd>⌫</kbd> deletes the selected sticker, <kbd>Esc</kbd> deselects, <kbd>⌘S</kbd> is **完成** (done).
